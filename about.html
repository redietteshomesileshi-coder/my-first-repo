About Glow Academy

A beauty academy built on science, creativity, and professional salon training in the heart of Adama.

Our mission is to elevate the beauty industry across Ethiopia through evidence-based education, practical lab training, and strong career support.

Our Story

Founded within the prestigious halls of Adama Science and Technology University, Glow Academy was created to bridge the gap between beauty artistry and scientific skincare. We empower students with practical salon skills, safety-first practices, and industry knowledge.

Students at Glow Academy don’t just learn makeup—they study dermatological chemistry, trichology, and salon business management so they can launch modern beauty careers.

Mission

Train confident professionals with science-backed beauty techniques and safety-first service.

Vision

Be the leading beauty education center in Ethiopia, advancing the salon industry with innovative training.

Values

Creativity, integrity, excellence, and student success guide everything we do.

Beauty Lab Training

Hands-on courses in makeup artistry, facial care, and skin therapy using modern clinical techniques.

Salon Entrepreneurship

We teach salon operations, client care, and digital marketing so graduates can succeed in business.
